## 📊 Overview
The data pipeline is designed to bridge high-frequency macroeconomic data parsing with real-time quantitative execution. Built with a modular architecture, it processes both traditional economic metrics and crypto market microstructure.

## 🔄 Data Ingestion & Processing Layers

1. **Macroeconomic Indicators (Fundamental Layer):**
   * Tracks multi-country inflation rates, central bank rate decisions, and global liquidity flows.
   * Feeds structural trend analysis into the AI inference engine.

2. **Order Book Microstructure (Technical Layer):**
   * Captures real-time tick data, bid-ask spreads, and depth-of-market metrics.
   * Operates with near-zero latency to detect short-term arbitrage and momentum opportunities.

3. **AI Inference & Closed-Loop Learning:**
   * Aggregated data is processed by proprietary models to generate execution signals.
   * Post-execution trade results (slippage, fill rate, P&L) are fed back into the system as training metadata, ensuring continuous self-optimization.
